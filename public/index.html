    Urgency (simple rules)

      How these rules work

      Red-flag wording (E1–E7, such as swelling of the lips or trouble breathing) gives Emergency wording. Any seriousness criterion, hospital care, a baby under 2, pregnancy or no recovery yet gives Priority review. Otherwise Routine. The rules are plain text matching: they never diagnose, and the model cannot change them.
